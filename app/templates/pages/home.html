Quick Reference Guide

Matrix Operations Quick Rules
  • Addition / Subtraction — matrices must share identical dimensions (\(m \times n\)).
  • Multiplication — inner dimensions must match: \(A_{m \times k} \times B_{k \times n} = C_{m \times n}\).
  • Inverse Matrix \(A^{-1}\) — defined only for square matrices where \(\det(A) \neq 0\).
  • Transpose \(A^T\) — swaps rows into columns such that \((A^T)_{ij} = A_{ji}\).
Vector Formulas Quick Reference
  • Magnitude — \(|\vec{v}| = \sqrt{v_1^2 + v_2^2 + \dots + v_n^2}\)
  • Dot Product — \(\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = |\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos(\theta)\)
  • Cross Product — 3D vectors only; yields a vector perpendicular to both.
  • Vector Projection — \(\text{proj}_{\vec{b}}(\vec{a}) = \left(\frac{\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b}}{|\vec{b}|^2}\right)\vec{b}\)
