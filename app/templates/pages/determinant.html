Quick Presets:
Key Properties of Determinants:
  • \(\det(A) \neq 0\) → matrix is invertible (non-singular).
  • \(\det(A) = 0\) → matrix is singular (no inverse).
  • \(|\det(A)|\) represents the volume scaling factor.
  • \(\det(AB) = \det(A) \times \det(B)\).
  • \(\det(A^T) = \det(A)\).
